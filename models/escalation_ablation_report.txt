Escalation ablation study
==========================

Test set: 118,108 transactions (4,064 fraud, 114,044 legit), replayed in chronological order through a fresh EntityRiskMemory (same order/state a live deployment would see).

=== Baseline: raw model score only, no entity escalation ===
Recall (frauds flagged REVIEW/BLOCK):    0.8578 (3,486 / 4,064)
False-flag rate (legit txns flagged):    0.0922 (10,515 / 114,044)

=== Escalation-adjusted: what the live system does today ===
Recall (frauds flagged REVIEW/BLOCK):    0.8873 (3,606 / 4,064)
False-flag rate (legit txns flagged):    0.1689 (19,266 / 114,044)

Recall delta from escalation:  +0.0295
False-flag-rate delta:         +0.0767

=== Precision of escalation-triggered flips ===
Of the transactions where entity escalation history actually pushed the action higher than the raw score alone would have (escalated_due_to_history == True):
  Flip count:  12,087
  Of those, actually fraud:  389
  Precision of escalation-triggered flips:  0.0322
